CASE STUDY 2024

Cyber Nexus

A deep-dive into the design and development of an immersive cyberpunk RPG environment, bridging high-fidelity aesthetics with seamless player interaction models.

ROLE
Lead UI/UX Designer
TIMELINE
6 Months
PLATFORM
Desktop / VR
FOCUS
Immersive UI

The Challenge & Process

Establishing the Core Identity

The primary goal was to create a user interface that didn't just sit on top of the world, but felt integrated within the 'Cyber Nexus' lore. We began with extensive user research into the mental models of RPG players, specifically focusing on how information density affects immersion during high-stakes combat and exploration.

Our iterative process involved over 50 prototype variants, testing everything from diegetic menus that appear as holographic projections from the player's wrist to minimal HUDs that respond to eye-tracking movements.

"The interface shouldn't feel like a layer. It should feel like the nervous system of the world itself." Project Vision Statement

Iterative Design & Testing

Using Figma and Unity, we developed a living design system. Every button press, window transition, and data visualization was stress-tested against different environmental lighting conditions to ensure accessibility remained a priority without sacrificing the neon-soaked cyberpunk aesthetic.

Technical & Creative Takeaways

Spatial Design

Mastered the transition from 2D screen-based layouts to 3D spatial UI that respects player depth perception.

Optimization

Developed custom shader workflows in Unity to render complex UI glows without impacting frame rates.

Accessibility

Implemented a high-contrast mode that preserves the cyberpunk vibe while ensuring readability for all users.
