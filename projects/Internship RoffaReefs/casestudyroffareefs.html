The Work & Process

About RoffaReefs

RoffaReefs is an organisation based on Bonaire dedicated to coral reef restoration and the study of local fish populations and their eggs. My internship combined two worlds: rigorous biological field work and the development of digital tools to make that research faster, more accurate, and easier to share with the people funding it.

Field Work & Data Collection

A large part of the internship was spent in and around the water. I collected fish eggs from different reef sites, took DNA samples, and recorded the biotic and abiotic factors that shape the local fish populations. Using genetic analysis I then matched each captured egg to the correct fish species, building up a reference dataset that didn't exist for the region before.

"Every egg you can identify is one more data point telling us how the reef is recovering." RoffaReefs research focus

Building the Identification Catalogue

The matched data became the foundation for an interactive fish (egg) identification catalogue: a tool that lets researchers and divers look up a species, see reference images, and quickly cross-check observations in the field. It replaces handwritten notebooks with a clean, searchable interface that anyone on the team can use.

Machine Learning Image Recognition

Using the same dataset, I trained a machine learning model that automatically links a photo of a fish egg to the most likely species. What used to take a trained eye and a lot of cross-referencing now happens in seconds, drastically lowering the barrier to identification work in the field.

Automation & Investor Dashboard

On top of the research tools I built small automations to streamline daily operations: scripts that fetch external data and drop it into the right files, and an informative dashboard visualising RoffaReefs' progress so the team can clearly show impact to investors and partners.

Fish Catalogue

A clear overview of all collected fish (egg) data, with filters that make identifying new eggs in the field a matter of seconds.

Automatic Data Fetcher

Pulls data from multiple sources automatically so repetitive tasks fall away and more time can be spent on the actual research.
